About the Legend

Lionel Andrés Messi, born on June 24, 1987, in Rosario, Argentina, is widely regarded as one of the greatest football players in history. Known for his extraordinary dribbling vision, playmaking brilliance, and lethal finishing, Messi has redefined football over a legendary career with FC Barcelona, Paris Saint-Germain, Inter Miami, and the Argentina national team.

Key Career Achievements

8× Ballon d'Or

The record holder for the most Ballon d'Or awards won in football history.

2022 World Cup Champion

Captained Argentina to FIFA World Cup glory in Qatar, winning the Golden Ball.

900+ Career Goals

A prolific goalscorer with over 900 official goals for club and country.

Notable Milestones

  • 2004: Made his official senior debut for FC Barcelona.
  • 2009: Won his first Ballon d'Or award and his first treble with Barcelona.
  • 2012: Set the world record for most goals scored in a single calendar year (91 goals).
  • 2021: Lifted his first major international trophy, winning the Copa América with Argentina.
  • 2022: Achieved his ultimate dream by winning the FIFA World Cup.
